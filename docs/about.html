About this project

What this is

Most hockey tables tell you what has happened. This project tries to tell you what is really going on underneath: which teams are genuinely good, which are getting lucky, and which are headed for trouble. It looks at things like the quality of shots a team creates and allows, how its goalie performs against the shots he faces, and how special teams are doing, then compares today's teams with teams from past seasons.

Every view is a hypothesis to test against history, not a fact. When the data disagrees with a popular hockey belief, this site will say so.

What is planned

  • Contender fitLive
    How closely each team's profile matches teams that reached the conference finals in past seasons, and why. Version 1 uses NHL team stats only; shot-quality data comes next.
  • Red flagsLive
    A simple checklist of warning signs, and how often teams with those signs have missed the playoffs.
  • Slow startersLive
    Which teams that began badly tend to recover, and what separated them from the ones that did not.
  • Goaltending in contextLive
    Whether a goalie is bailing out a weak defense or a strong defense is being let down, and whether a hot playoff goalie can be foreseen.
  • BreakoutsLive
    Whether teams that get out of their own end cleanly win more, measured with a breakout efficiency rating (BER) and other stats read from the play-by-play.
  • Zone time and chancesLive
    How much time each team spends in the offensive zone, and how many scoring chances it creates from that time.
  • Team profiles over timePlanned
    How each team's strengths and weaknesses change as the season goes on.

Reading the standings

GP
Games played.
W, L, OTL
Wins, regulation losses, and overtime or shootout losses.
PTS
Points: 2 for a win, 1 for an overtime or shootout loss.
P%
Points percentage: points won out of points available. Fair when teams have played different numbers of games.
ROW
Regulation plus overtime wins (shootout wins do not count). The first tiebreaker after points.
GF, GA, GD
Goals for, goals against, and the difference.
L10
Record over the last 10 games.
STRK
Current streak: W for wins, L for losses, OT for overtime losses.

Terms used on the research pages

Points %
Points won out of points available.
Corsi
Share of all shot attempts (shots on goal, misses and blocks) a team takes at 5-on-5. A measure of who controls play.
PDO
Shooting % plus save %. The league average is about 1.000, so a high PDO is usually good luck that tends to even out.
Expected goals
Each shot gets a probability of being a goal from where it was taken, the shot type and what happened before it. Adding them up measures shot quality.
GSAx
Goals saved above expected: the expected goals a goalie faced minus the goals he allowed.
High-danger shot
A shot from the most dangerous area of the ice (the slot), as defined by MoneyPuck.
Cut line, cushion
The cut line is the weakest team currently in a playoff spot. The cushion is how far above or below it a team is, in points %.
Percentile
Where a team ranks that season: 100 is the best in the league, 50 is average.
AUC
How well a ranking puts the teams that did something (such as reaching the conference finals) above the ones that did not. 1.0 is perfect, 0.5 is a coin flip.
Correlation
A number from -1 to +1 for how closely two things move together. About 0.3 is a modest link and 0.5 a strong one; it is usually worked out on ranks, so each season is compared fairly.
R-squared
The share of the differences between teams that a forecast explains. 0.25 means a quarter.
Lift
How much more often something happens to teams that meet a rule than to a random team.
Hold-out test
A rule is built on all seasons but one and tried on the one it has never seen, then repeated for each season.
Blind test
A few seasons are kept out of all the testing. The final recipe is frozen, then scored on them once.
Breakout attempt
A team wins the puck in its own zone at 5-on-5: a faceoff win, a takeaway, a blocked shot, or the opponent missing the net.
Controlled exit
A breakout where the team is next seen beyond its blue line still holding the puck.
Safe clear
A breakout where the puck gets out but the opponent has it, and it does not come straight back.
BER
Breakout efficiency rating: 100 × (controlled exits + half the safe clears) ÷ breakout attempts. Read from play-by-play events, so it is an approximation.
Scoring chance
A shot on goal, missed shot or goal at 5-on-5 from the area in front of the net: from the goal posts out to the faceoff dots, then out to the top of the circles.
Chance share
A team's scoring chances divided by all scoring chances in its games.
Zone time
The share of 5-on-5 time spent in each zone, from the NHL's EDGE puck tracking (2021-22 on).
BEI
Breakout efficiency index: own-zone giveaways per hour of own-zone time (45%), share of time in the own zone (30%) and transition shot attempts per 60 (25%), each as a league percentile. 0 to 100.
Pinned-in spell
The opponent gets two or more shot attempts in a row, each within 30 seconds of the last, without play leaving the zone or stopping.
Battle
A team's number minus its opponents' number in the same games: for example the pinned-in spells it suffers against the ones it inflicts.

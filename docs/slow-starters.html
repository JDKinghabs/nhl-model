What we found

The Thanksgiving claim
Of 222 teams out of a spot at Thanksgiving, 58 (26%) made the playoffs. It depends on how far behind: just out 42% (24 of 57), behind 33% (18 of 55), far behind 14% (16 of 110). So 'rarely recover' is true for teams well behind and not for teams just out.
What it takes
Pace needed over the rest of the season to reach the typical final cut line (about 0.574 points %): needing under .600: 41% (34 of 83); needing .600 to .650: 20% (23 of 116); needing .650 to .700: 4% (1 of 22); needing .700 or more: 0% (0 of 1).
Signs of recovery
No sign is consistent at all four checkpoints. Hints (small samples): Strong shot share (Corsi), Strong special teams. No clear effect: Running cold (PDO), Goals say better than record, Stays out of the box, Hot lately. With 24 sign-and-checkpoint tests, a few results this strong can happen by chance: read them as 'worth a look'.
Stacking (Thanksgiving)
0 signs: 19 of 75 (25%); 1 sign: 18 of 75 (24%); 2 signs: 11 of 49 (22%); 3 or more signs: 10 of 23 (43%).
Honest test
Adding the number of signs to the distance behind changes the hold-out AUC by +0.004 on average (margin about +/-0.022): within the noise, so the signs do not clearly improve on simply asking how far behind the team was.
Who recovered
58 teams out of a spot at Thanksgiving made it; 5 of them reached the Cup final, including St. Louis Blues (2018-19) who won the Cup.
How to use it
Treat a slow start as a reason to check HOW far behind the team is and what pace it needs, then look at the signs for whether its record undersells it. Early in a season the signs are noisy.

Signs of a coming recovery

Six signs, fixed in advance as the top or bottom quarter of the league: the mirror image of the red flags. "Most consistent" would mean teams with the sign recovered more than equally far behind teams at every checkpoint.

How it was tested, and the limits

  • Each team with a sign is compared with teams the same distance behind, so a sign cannot win just by meaning "only slightly behind".
  • With 6 signs at 4 checkpoints, a few results will look interesting by chance. The groups are small (about 220 teams per checkpoint, 50 to 70 recoveries).
  • Team statistics only: no injuries, trades, schedule or goalie changes.

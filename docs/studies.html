Studies

Each study asks one hockey question of up to 15 seasons of NHL data (2009-10 on), tests the answer on seasons it has not seen, and says plainly when the evidence is thin.

Every page has a team-by-team list and a plain-English summary of what the history says. The pages are estimates from past patterns, not predictions or betting advice.

  • Contender fitLive
    Does a score built from NHL team stats pick the real conference finalists better than just ranking by points %?
    Headline: Slightly, but not provably.
  • Red flagsLive
    Being in a playoff spot is not the same as being safe: do six warning signs make a team that is in more likely to miss?
    Headline: Of the teams in a spot at Thanksgiving, 24% missed; at Jan 1, 20%; at the trade deadline, 10%.
  • Slow startersLive
    A team out of a playoff spot at Thanksgiving: how often does it recover, what does it take, and is there any sign of which ones will?
    Headline: Of 222 teams out of a spot at Thanksgiving, 58 (26%) made the playoffs.
  • Goaltending in contextLive
    Is a goalie bailing out a weak defense, or a strong defense being let down by its goalie? And can anything foresee a hot playoff goalie?
    Headline: Defense repeats from before Jan 1 to after (correlation 0.65 for expected goals against); goaltending results repeat much less (0.18 for goals saved above expected, 0.31 for raw save %).
  • BreakoutsLive
    Do teams that get out of their own end cleanly win more, and is that anything more than out-shooting the opponent?
    Headline: Teams that get out of their own end well do win more, but breaking out is hard to tell apart from simply out-shooting the opponent.
